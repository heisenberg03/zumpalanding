How It Works

Simple steps,
unforgettable results

1

Post Your Gig

Describe your event — the vibe, date, venue, and budget. Go live in under five minutes.

2

Receive Applications

Verified artists apply directly. Review profiles, portfolios, and ratings at a glance.

3

Invite Who You Want

Spotted the perfect artist? Send a personal invite they can accept with one tap.

4

Book & Manage

Confirm bookings and coordinate via in-app messaging — your entire lineup in one place.

1

Build Your Profile

Turn On Pro Mode

3

Browse & Apply

App Features

Everything in one place —
no middlemen required

📋

Gig Management

Post, edit, and manage gigs with full visibility over applications, invites, and confirmed bookings.

🌟

Professional Mode

Artists toggle visibility on demand. Appear in discovery listings only when you're available for bookings.

🖼️

Portfolio Showcase

Upload performance videos and photos to your profile so hosts can see exactly what they're booking.

⭐

Dual Ratings

Hosts and artists are rated separately — as a professional and as a host — building trust on both sides.
