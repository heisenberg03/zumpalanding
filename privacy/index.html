Zumpa ("Zumpa," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to the responsible and lawful processing of personal data. This Privacy Policy ("Policy") sets forth the manner in which personal data is collected, processed, stored, disclosed, and protected in connection with your use of the Zumpa mobile application ("Application"). This Policy is issued in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), the Information Technology Act, 2000 ("IT Act"), and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 ("SPDI Rules"). This Policy governs only users located within the territory of India. Use of the Application outside India is not authorised.

§1. Information Collected

1.1 Zumpa collects only such personal data as is strictly necessary for the lawful operation of the Application. The categories of data collected are as follows:

1.2 Venue or location details associated with gig listings are entered manually by users and do not constitute real-time device location data.

§2. Purpose of Processing

2.1 Personal data shall be processed exclusively for the following purposes and shall not be processed for any purpose beyond those specified herein:

  • Authentication and Identity Verification: Phone numbers are transmitted to msg91 (operating under TRAI/DLT regulations) solely for OTP-based authentication. No password is stored.
  • Service Delivery: To display user profiles and gig listings to relevant parties, facilitate in-application messaging, and deliver push notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Security and Fraud Prevention: Server logs, including IP addresses, request timestamps, and API metadata, are retained for a period not exceeding ninety (90) days and are used exclusively to detect and investigate misuse, unauthorised access, and security incidents. Such logs shall not be used for profiling, analytics, or advertising under any circumstances.
  • Legal Compliance: To respond to lawfully issued orders, directions, or requests from courts or competent government authorities under applicable Indian law.

2.2 Zumpa does not process personal data for advertising, targeted marketing, behavioural profiling, sale or commercialisation to third parties, or automated decision-making of any nature.

2.3 Legal Basis: All processing is conducted on the basis of explicit, informed consent obtained from the Data Principal at the time of registration. Consent may be withdrawn at any time in accordance with Section 5 of this Policy.

§3. Disclosure of Information

3.1 Zumpa does not sell, rent, trade, lease, or otherwise transfer personal data to any third party for commercial purposes. Disclosure is strictly limited to the following categories of recipients, each bound by appropriate data processing agreements:

  • msg91 / VI DLT: Processes phone numbers solely for OTP transmission, in compliance with TRAI and DLT regulatory requirements.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — Google LLC: Processes device push notification tokens solely for the delivery of in-application notifications.
  • Cloudflare R2: Stores user-uploaded profile images and portfolio media.
  • Database Hosting Provider: Hosts encrypted PostgreSQL databases containing user account data.
  • Other Users of the Application: Profile information and gig listings are displayed to other users as necessary for platform functionality. Users retain full control over the content they include in their profiles.
  • Competent Legal Authorities: Personal data may be disclosed pursuant to a valid court order, government direction, or statutory obligation under applicable Indian law. Where disclosure is not prohibited by law, the affected Data Principal shall be notified.

3.2 No personal data is disclosed to advertising networks, analytics providers, data brokers, or any other commercial third party.

§4. Data Storage, Security, and Cross-Border Transfers

4.1 Storage: All personal data is stored in encrypted PostgreSQL databases subject to access controls, role-based authentication, and periodic security audits.

4.2 Security Measures: Zumpa implements the following technical and organisational safeguards: (i) encryption of data in transit via TLS/HTTPS and at rest where applicable; (ii) restriction of access to personal data to authorised personnel only, on a need-to-know basis; (iii) periodic review and assessment of security practices and infrastructure; and (iv) OTP-based authentication eliminating the need to store passwords.

4.3 Cross-Border Transfers: Certain service providers, including Cloudflare, Inc. and Google LLC (Firebase), may process or store data on servers located outside the territory of India. Zumpa ensures that all such cross-border transfers are subject to data processing agreements that provide a standard of protection materially equivalent to that required under Indian data protection law. By using the Application, the Data Principal consents to such transfers to the extent necessary for service provision. Zumpa shall update its infrastructure as required to comply with any data localisation mandates issued under the DPDP Act or subordinate rules.

4.4 Breach Notification: In the event of a personal data breach, Zumpa shall, within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of such breach, notify affected Data Principals and report the incident to the Data Protection Board of India as required under the DPDP Act, and shall take immediate remedial measures to contain and address the breach.

§5. Rights of Data Principals

5.1 In accordance with the DPDP Act, every Data Principal is entitled to exercise the following rights with respect to their personal data:

  • Right of Access (§11 DPDP Act): To obtain a summary of personal data processed by Zumpa and information regarding the processing activities undertaken in relation to such data.
  • Right of Correction and Updation (§12 DPDP Act): To have inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated personal data corrected or updated.
  • Right of Erasure (§12 DPDP Act): To request deletion of personal data and the associated account, subject to applicable legal retention obligations.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (§6 DPDP Act): To withdraw previously granted consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent may result in inability to access or use the Application, as core functionality is dependent on data processing.
  • Right of Nomination (§14 DPDP Act): To nominate an individual authorised to exercise data rights in the event of the Data Principal's death or incapacity.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal (§13 DPDP Act): To lodge a complaint with the Grievance Officer and, if unsatisfied with the resolution, to escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.

5.2 To exercise any of the foregoing rights, the Data Principal shall submit a request to the Grievance Officer at support@zumpa.in. Zumpa shall acknowledge the request and respond within thirty (30) days of receipt.

§6. Retention Periods

6.1 Personal data shall be retained only for the period necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, subject to the following schedules:

  • Account Data (name, phone number, profile information, gig listings): Retained for the duration of account activity. Deleted within thirty (30) days of a valid account deletion request.
  • Security Logs (IP addresses, timestamps, API metadata): Retained for ninety (90) days from the date of collection, after which they are permanently and irreversibly deleted.
  • In-Application Chat Messages: Retained for the duration of account activity. Deleted within thirty (30) days of account deletion.
  • Legally Mandated Retention: Data subject to retention obligations under applicable Indian law — including but not limited to the Income Tax Act, 1961 — may be retained for a period of up to seven (7) years.
  • Anonymised and Aggregated Data: Data rendered incapable of identifying any individual may be retained for an indefinite period.

§7. Minors

7.1 The Application is intended exclusively for individuals who have attained the age of eighteen (18) years. Zumpa does not knowingly collect or process personal data from individuals below the age of majority. Upon discovery of any such processing, Zumpa shall forthwith terminate the associated account and delete all personal data collected in connection therewith. Parents or legal guardians who become aware of unauthorised use of the Application by a minor are requested to notify Zumpa at support@zumpa.in.

§8. Amendments to This Policy

8.1 Zumpa reserves the right to amend this Policy at any time to reflect changes in applicable law, regulatory guidance, or operational practices. Material amendments shall be communicated to Data Principals through a notice within the Application prior to the amended Policy taking effect. Continued use of the Application following notification of such amendments shall constitute acceptance of the revised Policy. Data Principals who do not accept the amended Policy are required to cease use of the Application and submit a request for account deletion.

§9. Grievance Officer

9.1 In accordance with Rule 5(9) of the SPDI Rules and the DPDP Act, Zumpa has designated a Grievance Officer to receive and address complaints and requests from Data Principals:

Grievance Officer: Support Team, Zumpa
Email: support@zumpa.in
Address: Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India

9.2 Zumpa shall acknowledge all complaints within twenty-four (24) hours of receipt and endeavour to resolve them within thirty (30) days, in compliance with the IT Rules and the DPDP Act. Data Principals who remain unsatisfied with the resolution may escalate their complaint to the Data Protection Board of India in accordance with the DPDP Act.
