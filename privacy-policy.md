# Zumpa Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: March 15, 2026**

Zumpa ("Zumpa," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you use our mobile application ("App"). We comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), the Information Technology Act, 2000 ("IT Act"), and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

This Policy applies only to users in India. The App is intended for use within India only.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to operate the App:

**Information you provide:**
- Phone number (required — used for OTP-based authentication via msg91/VI DLT)
- Email address (optional — if you choose to provide it)
- Username, full name, bio, profile picture
- Professional type and skill categories
- Gig descriptions, portfolios, and venue/location details for gigs (e.g., addresses, latitude/longitude if you provide them)
- Messages sent through in-app chat
- Social media links (e.g., YouTube, Instagram — only if you voluntarily link them)

**Information collected automatically:**
- Device push notification token (for sending notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging)
- Device OS type (Android/iOS) and app version
- Server-side logs: IP address, timestamps, and API request metadata (for security only)

**Information we do NOT collect:**
- Financial or payment information (no payment feature exists)
- Sensitive personal data such as biometric data, health data, caste, religion, sexual orientation, or political affiliation
- IMEI, MAC address, advertising ID, or persistent device identifiers
- Location data from your device (GPS/background location) — we only store venue addresses you manually enter for gigs
- Data from third-party sources (we do not purchase or obtain data from data brokers)
- Cookies or web tracking technologies

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely for:
- **Authentication**: Verifying your identity via OTP (phone number processed by msg91 in compliance with TRAI/DLT regulations).
- **Providing the App**: Displaying your profile to relevant users, showing gig listings, enabling in-app messaging and notifications.
- **Security and fraud prevention**: Server logs (IP addresses, timestamps, API metadata) are used exclusively to detect misuse, prevent unauthorised access, and investigate security incidents. Logs are retained for 90 days and then deleted. They are never used for profiling, advertising, or analytics.
- **Legal compliance**: Responding to lawful requests from government authorities or courts.

We do NOT use your data for:
- Advertising, targeted marketing, or behavioural profiling.
- Selling, renting, or trading to any third party.
- Automated decision-making or profiling.
- Any purpose not listed above.

**Legal basis for processing:** We process your data based on your explicit consent obtained during sign-up. You may withdraw consent at any time (see Section 5).

## 3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data for commercial purposes. Sharing is strictly limited to:

- **Service providers** (bound by data processing agreements):
  - msg91 / VI DLT — SMS delivery for OTP (processes your phone number)
  - Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — push notifications (processes your device token)
  - Cloudflare R2 — file storage for profile pictures and portfolio media
  - Our database hosting provider — stores your account data in encrypted Postgres databases

- **Other users**: Your profile information and gig listings are visible to other users of the App as necessary for the platform to function. You control what information you include in your profile.

- **Legal authorities**: We will disclose data if required by a valid court order, government direction, or to comply with applicable Indian law. We will notify you of such disclosure unless prohibited by law.

We do not share data with advertisers, analytics providers, or data brokers.

## 4. Data Storage, Security, and Cross-Border Transfers

**Storage:** Your data is stored in encrypted PostgreSQL databases with access controls, authentication, and regular security reviews.

**Security measures:**
- Encryption of data in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest where applicable.
- Access to personal data is restricted to authorised personnel only.
- Regular review of security practices.
- No storage of passwords (authentication is OTP-based).

**Cross-border transfers:** Some of our service providers (Cloudflare, Firebase/Google) may process or store data on servers located outside India. We ensure such transfers are governed by data processing agreements that provide protection equivalent to Indian data protection law. By using the App, you consent to such transfers as necessary to provide the service. We will update our infrastructure to comply with any data localisation requirements imposed under the DPDP Act or related rules.

**Data breach notification:** In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify affected users and the Data Protection Board of India within 72 hours as required under the DPDP Act, and take immediate steps to contain and remediate the breach.

## 5. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:
- **Access** your personal data held by us.
- **Correct or update** inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erase** your data by requesting account deletion.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time — this may result in loss of access to the App since core features require data processing.
- **Nominate** a person to exercise your data rights in case of your death or incapacity.
- **Grievance redressal** — file a complaint with our Grievance Officer or escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any right, contact our Grievance Officer at support@zumpa.in. We will respond within 30 days.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Account data** (name, phone, profile, listings): Retained while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion request.
- **Server logs** (IP, timestamps, API metadata): Retained for 90 days, then permanently deleted.
- **Chat messages**: Retained while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
- **Legal/regulatory obligations**: Data required under applicable Indian law (e.g., tax records under Income Tax Act) may be retained for up to 7 years.
- **Anonymised/aggregated data**: May be retained indefinitely as it cannot identify you.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we become aware of underage use, we will immediately terminate the account and delete all associated data. Parents or guardians who discover unauthorised use by a minor should contact us at support@zumpa.in.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. Material changes will be notified through the App. Continued use after notification constitutes acceptance. If you do not agree, you must stop using the App and request account deletion.

## 10. Business Transfers

In the event that Zumpa is involved in a merger, acquisition, asset sale, restructuring, or any similar transaction, personal data held by Zumpa may be transferred to the acquiring or successor entity as part of that transaction. We will notify affected users through the App before their personal data becomes subject to a materially different privacy policy. If you do not consent to the transfer, you may request account deletion prior to the effective date of the transaction by contacting us at support@zumpa.in.

## 11. Third-Party Links

The Application may display links to third-party websites or services — for example, social media profiles voluntarily linked by users (such as YouTube or Instagram channels). Zumpa has no control over, and assumes no responsibility for, the content, privacy practices, or data handling of any third-party site or service. Accessing such links will direct you away from the Application and into the third party's data environment. We strongly encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party service before providing personal information to it.
