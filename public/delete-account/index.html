Delete Your Account

Account & Data Deletion · Applies to: Zumpa Mobile Application

Contents

  • §2 Delete by Email

You can request deletion of your Zumpa account and all associated personal data at any time — whether or not you still have access to the app. Two options are available: directly from within the app, or by sending an email to our support team. All deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.

§1. Delete In-App

If you have access to the Zumpa app, account deletion can be initiated directly from your profile settings:

  1. Open the Zumpa app and go to your Profile
  2. Tap Settings
  3. Select Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Your account and data will be permanently removed within 7 business days of confirmation.

§2. Delete by Email

If you no longer have access to the app, you can submit a deletion request by email:

Email: support@zumpa.in
Subject: Delete My Zumpa Account
Include: The phone number linked to your account
Processing time: Within 7 business days of receiving your request

We will send a confirmation once your account has been deleted.

§3. What Gets Deleted

Upon processing your deletion request, the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

  • Profile information — name, phone number, bio, photo, and preferences
  • Gig listings you have created
  • Booking history and in-app chat messages
  • Reviews and ratings associated with your account

§4. Data Retention

Some records may be retained beyond the deletion date where required by law:

  • Security logs (IP addresses, timestamps) are retained for up to 90 days from the date of collection and are then permanently deleted — this retention period is unaffected by account deletion.
  • Legal and transaction records may be kept for up to 7 years where required under applicable Indian law (e.g. Income Tax Act, 1961).

For full details on how your data is handled, please review our Privacy Policy.
